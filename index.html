giovanni almeida studied for a brief period at the university of art and design lausanne (écal) in switzerland. since his return to brazil, he has been working as a graphic designer for thiago lacaz.

with a practice rooted in investigating and questioning the language defined by the swiss style, his work includes visual identities, posters, type and editorial design.
